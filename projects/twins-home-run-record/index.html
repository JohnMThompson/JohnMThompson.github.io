Minnesota Twins Home Run Record

A Tableau visualization tracking the Minnesota Twins as they raced toward the 2019 MLB home-run record.

  • Tableau
  • Data Analysis
  • Data Visualization
Open visualization

As the Minnesota Twins approached the MLB team home-run record in 2019, I scraped historical statistics from Baseball-Reference with KNIME and built a Tableau dashboard to compare their pace with prior seasons.
